About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Dallas, advised by Prof. Yunhui Guo. Prior to my Ph.D., I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence and B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Seoul. During my M.S., I was advised by Prof. Kyungwoo Song at Yonsei University and Prof. Jiyoung Jung at the University of Seoul.

My research focuses on building reliable and robust artificial intelligence systems. I currently study how Vision-Language Models (VLMs) generalize and adapt in open-world, non-stationary, and distributionally shifting environments.

Projects

Ongoing

  • Since Sep 2026

    Open-Set Test-Time Adaptation of Vision–Language Models for Object Detection

    Source-free adaptation for reliable detection under distribution shift, preserving known-class recognition while localizing unfamiliar objects and explicitly distinguishing them from known categories and background.

  • Since Oct 2026

    Continual Learning for Long-Sequence Video QA with Hybrid Mamba

    Robust continual learning for long-sequence video QA, using the context-retention capabilities of hybrid Mamba architectures to preserve long-range temporal dependencies and mitigate forgetting across tasks.

  • Since Aug 2026

    Psychological Memory for Long-Term Conversational Agents

    Agentic construction of human-grounded datasets and benchmarks to evaluate how long-term conversational agents integrate individual traits and evolving psychological states into personalized, supportive responses.

Past

  • Feb 2025 – Aug 2025

    Failure-Aware Causal Modeling through FMEA-Driven LLM Adaptation

    Leveraging domain-specific technical documents from the automotive industry to pretrain and adapt LLMs for generating accurate, component-targeted FMEA scenarios via causal reasoning.

  • May 2024 – Oct 2024

    Causal Discovery in Photoplethysmography Attributes

    Uncovering causal relationships among PPG signals and diverse physiological attributes (e.g., race, age) to enable robust and accurate arterial blood pressure (ABP) estimation.
